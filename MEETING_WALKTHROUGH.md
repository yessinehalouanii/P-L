# STEPS Meeting Walkthrough — 6 minutes

## 0:00–0:45 — Executive Command
“This is no longer another reporting dashboard. The idea is to bring the hotel’s existing data into one consulting intelligence layer. We can see financial performance, revenue, labour, guest experience, people, operations, asset risk and sales together, then translate those signals into decisions.”

Show Total Revenue, GOP, Occupancy and Labour % Revenue. Point to the highest-priority consultant finding.

## 0:45–1:45 — P&L Intelligence
Open **P&L Intelligence**.

“This is the financial backbone. A hotel can upload its own P&L in Excel/CSV or enter lines manually. We normalize the data into Actual, Budget and Prior Year, then analyze departmental profit, GOP, EBITDA and the largest variances.”

Click **Variance drivers**.

“The important part is that we do not stop at ‘F&B payroll is over budget’. We link that variance to POS covers, payroll hours and guest feedback to understand why.”

## 1:45–2:45 — Data Center
Open **Data Center**.

“STEPS does not replace OPERA, a POS, payroll or accounting. It sits above them. Each client can export its existing data. We support P&L, PMS, RMS, STR/CoStar, F&B POS, Payroll, HR, Guest Feedback, CMMS, Utilities, CRM, Website/Booking Engine and STEPS consultant evidence.”

Click **View fields** on P&L, PMS and F&B POS. Then show **Upload CSV/XLSX** and **Manual entry**.

“The same architecture works whether a property uses OPERA, Mews, Cloudbeds, Simphony, Lightspeed, ADP or another system, because STEPS maps the exported fields into a common model.”

## 2:45–3:35 — Labour Optimization
Open **Labour Optimization**.

“This is aligned with STEPS AI Labour: demand, staffing, hours, payroll and profitability. But there is a service guardrail. The system should never say ‘cut labour’ simply because payroll is high. It checks guest and operational evidence first.”

Point out Payroll variance and Hours / Occupied Room.

## 3:35–4:25 — Operational Efficiency / F&B
Open **Operational Efficiency**.

“For F&B, finance alone tells us margin is weak. POS tells us covers, average cheque and food cost. Payroll tells us labour deployment. Guest feedback tells us whether service is suffering. That combination is what turns a number into an operational recommendation.”

## 4:25–5:10 — Mystery Shop
Open **Mystery Shop**.

“This is the STEPS-specific layer that generic BI software does not have. The consultant can capture standards, scores, observations, severity, evidence and recommendations from the operational audit, then connect them to the quantitative hotel data.”

## 5:10–6:00 — Recommendations & Actions
Open **Recommendations & Actions**.

“The workflow is Evidence → Finding → Recommendation → STEPS approval → SMART action. AI informs; the consultant decides. Every action has an owner, due date, measurable target and guest/service guardrail. Then the Owner Review page turns the work into a decision pack.”

Finish with:

“The opportunity I see is not to replace hotel systems. It is to productize the STEPS methodology above those systems so every consultant can diagnose faster, explain the why more clearly and track whether the recommendation actually worked.”
